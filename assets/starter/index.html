Adapt this starter

  1. Replace SUBJECT_POLYGON or isInsideSubjectShape() to change a wrapped silhouette.
  2. Replace materialPointToWorld() when the subject needs another carrier, such as a tube, helix, or shell.
  3. Keep worldPointToView() and colorFromSignedViewDepth() after the object transform.

The invariant

Material coordinates identify each particle for the full loop. The renderer recomputes view.z and display color on every frame. Do not store the sampled color on the particle.

Camera rule

The starter rotates around world y, tilts the camera around x, and projects view.x and view.y orthographically. It keeps view.z for the color field.
